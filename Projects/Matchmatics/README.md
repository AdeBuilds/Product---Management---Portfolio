# Matchmatics

## Product Overview

Matchmatics is a product concept focused on helping intentional adults find meaningful relationships with marriage as the goal.

## Product Focus

The product is designed around intentional dating, meaningful connections, and helping users find compatible partners with serious relationship goals.
