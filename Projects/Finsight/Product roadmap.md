# FinSight — Product Roadmap

## Roadmap Objective

The FinSight roadmap outlines the planned development of the product from the initial Minimum Viable Product (MVP) to future improvements.

## Phase 1 — Discovery & Validation

### Goal
Validate the problem and understand the needs of SME owners.

### Activities

- Conduct user interviews.
- Conduct surveys.
- Analyse existing financial management solutions.
- Identify major SME financial pain points.
- Validate the proposed solution.

## Phase 2 — MVP

### Goal
Launch the core FinSight experience.

### Features

- User registration
- Financial information input
- Financial health assessment
- Financial health score
- Basic financial dashboard
- Risk indicators
- Basic recommendations

### Success Focus

Validate whether SME owners can understand their financial health and find the insights useful.

## Phase 3 — Product Improvement

### Goal
Improve engagement and provide deeper financial insights.

### Features

- Financial health history
- Detailed financial insights
- Customizable financial reports
- Educational financial resources
- Personalized financial improvement plans

## Phase 4 — Advanced Features

### Goal
Expand FinSight into a more comprehensive financial management solution.

### Features

- Financial forecasting
- Budgeting tools
- Business performance comparisons
- AI-powered financial recommendations
- Automated financial reports

## Phase 5 — Integrations

### Goal
Reduce manual data entry and improve the accuracy of financial information.

### Potential Integrations

- Accounting platforms
- Payment platforms
- Banking services

## Prioritization Principles

Features will be prioritized based on:

- User impact
- Business value
- Technical feasibility
- User feedback
- Product metrics
- Research findings

## Roadmap Review

The roadmap will be reviewed regularly as new user research, product data, and business requirements become available.

The roadmap is flexible and may change based on validated user needs and product performance.
