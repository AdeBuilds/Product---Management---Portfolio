# FinSight — Product Metrics & KPIs

## Product Objective

The primary objective of FinSight is to help SME owners understand their financial health and take informed actions based on the insights provided.

## North Star Metric

### Percentage of Active Users Who Complete a Financial Health Assessment

This metric measures whether users are actively using FinSight to understand their business financial health.

## Acquisition Metrics

- Number of new users
- User registration rate
- Cost per acquired user
- Percentage of visitors who sign up

## Activation Metrics

- Percentage of users who complete their first assessment
- Time taken to complete the first assessment
- Percentage of users who view their financial health score

## Engagement Metrics

- Monthly Active Users (MAU)
- Weekly Active Users (WAU)
- Number of financial assessments completed
- Average number of assessments per active user
- Frequency of dashboard visits

## Retention Metrics

- 7-day retention rate
- 30-day retention rate
- Percentage of users who return to complete another assessment

## Feature Adoption Metrics

- Percentage of users who use the financial dashboard
- Percentage of users who view recommendations
- Percentage of users who view risk indicators
- Percentage of users who update their financial information

## Outcome Metrics

- Percentage of users who report taking action based on recommendations
- Improvement in users' financial health scores over time
- User-reported confidence in making financial decisions
- User satisfaction score

## User Experience Metrics

- Customer Satisfaction Score (CSAT)
- Net Promoter Score (NPS)
- User feedback rating
- Support request rate

## Success Criteria

FinSight will be considered successful if users:

- Complete financial assessments regularly.
- Understand their financial health score.
- Engage with recommendations.
- Return to monitor their financial health.
- Report increased confidence in making financial decisions.

## Measurement Approach

Metrics will be monitored through product analytics, user surveys, feedback, and periodic user research.

The metrics will be reviewed regularly to identify opportunities for product improvement.
