# FinSight

## SME Financial Health Diagnostic

### Product Overview

FinSight is a financial health diagnostic tool designed to help Small and Medium-sized Enterprises (SMEs) understand their financial position and make better-informed business decisions.

### Problem

Many SMEs struggle to understand their financial health because financial information can be difficult to interpret, scattered across different records, or not readily available in a useful format.

This makes it difficult for business owners to identify financial problems early and make informed decisions.

### Target Users

- Small and medium-sized business owners
- Entrepreneurs
- SME managers
- Business operators who need a simple way to understand their financial health

### Proposed Solution

FinSight provides SMEs with a simple way to assess their financial health by analysing key business information and presenting useful insights in an easy-to-understand format.

### Key Features

- Financial health assessment
- Financial data input
- Financial health score
- Business insights and recommendations
- Financial performance dashboard
- Risk identification

### Product Goals

1. Help SMEs understand their financial health.
2. Identify potential financial risks early.
3. Provide actionable insights for better decision-making.
4. Make financial analysis easier for non-financial business owners.

### Key Metrics

- Number of SMEs completing financial assessments
- Assessment completion rate
- Monthly active users
- User retention rate
- Percentage of users who act on recommendations
- User satisfaction score
