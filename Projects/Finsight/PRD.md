# FinSight — Product Requirements Document

## 1. Product Overview

FinSight is a financial health diagnostic tool designed to help Small and Medium-sized Enterprises (SMEs) understand their financial position and make better-informed business decisions.

## 2. Problem Statement

Many SME owners struggle to understand their business financial health because financial information can be difficult to interpret and may be scattered across different records.

As a result, business owners may not identify financial risks early enough or have the information they need to make confident decisions.

## 3. Product Goal

To provide SME owners with a simple and accessible tool that assesses their financial health and provides actionable insights based on their financial information.

## 4. Target Users

- Small and medium-sized business owners
- Entrepreneurs
- SME managers
- Business operators without extensive financial expertise

## 5. User Needs

Users need to:

- Understand the current financial health of their business.
- Identify potential financial risks.
- Understand important financial indicators.
- Receive simple and actionable recommendations.
- Track changes in their financial health over time.

## 6. Key Features

### Financial Assessment

Users can enter relevant financial information about their business.

### Financial Health Score

The system provides an overall financial health score based on the information provided.

### Financial Dashboard

Users can view important financial indicators and their overall financial position.

### Insights and Recommendations

The product provides simple explanations and recommendations based on the user's financial information.

### Risk Identification

The system highlights potential financial risks that may require the user's attention.

## 7. Functional Requirements

- Users should be able to create an account.
- Users should be able to enter their business financial information.
- The system should calculate a financial health score.
- Users should be able to view their financial dashboard.
- The system should provide relevant insights.
- The system should identify potential financial risks.
- Users should be able to update their financial information.

## 8. Success Metrics

- Assessment completion rate
- Number of completed financial assessments
- Monthly active users
- User retention rate
- User satisfaction score
- Percentage of users who act on recommendations

## 9. MVP Scope

The Minimum Viable Product will include:

- User registration
- Financial information input
- Financial health assessment
- Financial health score
- Basic dashboard
- Basic recommendations
- Risk indicators

## 10. Future Improvements

Future versions could include:

- Financial forecasting
- Automated financial reports
- Budgeting tools
- Business performance comparisons
- AI-powered financial recommendations
- Integration with accounting platforms

## 11. Product Assumptions

- SME owners are willing to provide basic financial information.
- Users prefer simple financial insights rather than complex financial reports.
- Users have access to basic records of their business finances.

## 12. Risks

- Users may provide inaccurate financial information.
- Some SME owners may be uncomfortable sharing financial data.
- Users may not understand financial recommendations without additional explanations.
