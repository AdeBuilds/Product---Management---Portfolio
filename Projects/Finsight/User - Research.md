# FinSight — User Research

## Research Objective

The objective of the research is to understand the financial challenges faced by SME owners and identify the information and tools they need to make better financial decisions.

## Research Questions

- How do SME owners currently track their business finances?
- What financial challenges do they experience?
- Which financial indicators do they understand or struggle with?
- How do they currently identify financial risks?
- What prevents them from making informed financial decisions?
- What would make a financial health tool useful to them?

## Target Participants

The research would focus on:

- Small business owners
- SME managers
- Entrepreneurs
- Business operators responsible for financial decisions

## Research Methods

### User Interviews

Conduct interviews with SME owners to understand their experiences, challenges, and financial decision-making processes.

### Surveys

Use surveys to collect quantitative information about common financial challenges and current financial management practices.

### Competitor Research

Analyse existing financial management and business intelligence tools to identify gaps and opportunities.

## Key Hypotheses

- SME owners need simpler ways to understand financial information.
- Many SME owners do not have access to dedicated financial analysts.
- SME owners would value simple financial health indicators.
- Actionable recommendations could help users make better financial decisions.

## Expected Insights

The research is expected to provide insights into:

- The most common financial challenges faced by SMEs.
- The financial information users consider most important.
- The barriers preventing SMEs from monitoring their financial health.
- The features users would find most valuable.

## Research Impact on Product

Research findings will be used to:

- Validate the problem.
- Prioritize product features.
- Improve the user experience.
- Refine the financial health assessment.
- Develop more relevant recommendations.

## Next Steps

1. Recruit SME participants.
2. Conduct user interviews.
3. Distribute a survey.
4. Analyse research findings.
5. Identify common themes and pain points.
6. Translate insights into product requirements.
7. Validate the proposed solution with users.
